Amazon · Cloud migration

Zero-downtime move to AWS Lambda

Problem
An important order service ran on older servers and Java 8.
What I did
Redesigned it on AWS Lambda (Java 21) with ordered queues, retries and a safety net for failed messages, then switched traffic over gradually.
Result
1,200+ events per minute, zero downtime, rollback ready in under 10 minutes.
